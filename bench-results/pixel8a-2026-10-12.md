# Pixel 8a: streaming and mount results (2026-10-12)

First run of the full benchmark matrix on Android, on a physical device.

- **Device:** Pixel 8a, Android 17 (API 37), 120 Hz, Release build (arm64-v8a), cabled, charging.
- **Code:** `402786b`. The stream server on the Mac, reached through `adb reverse tcp:9877 tcp:9877`.
- **Clock:** the phone's clock is not the Mac's. Each streaming run measures its offset to the server first
  (`GET /time`, NTP-style: about −300 to −450 ms here, over a 15–30 ms round trip), and every delay is
  corrected by it.
- **Raw data:** [`bench-bubble-pixel8a-2026-10-12T14-14-48-815Z.json`](bench-bubble-pixel8a-2026-10-12T14-14-48-815Z.json),
  [`bench-block-pixel8a-2026-10-12T14-52-32-893Z.json`](bench-block-pixel8a-2026-10-12T14-52-32-893Z.json),
  [`mount-pixel8a-2026-10-12T15-02-25-376Z.json`](mount-pixel8a-2026-10-12T15-02-25-376Z.json).
- Contenders and columns as in the [README](../README.md#benchmarks). A stutter is a frame over 1.5× its
  budget; at 120 Hz the budget is 8.3 ms, so a 17 ms frame is one stutter here (it isn't at 60 Hz).

## Takeaways

- **Streaming into a chat bubble: 10–17 ms typical, 19–26 ms worst 5%**, for a 5 KB or a 100 KB answer, with
  long paragraphs or one 100 KB paragraph, and with the JS thread busy. The iOS simulator: 11–16 / 17–23.
- **The JS contenders fall behind on a long answer:** 72–659 ms typical and 193–2730 ms worst 5%, with
  51–146 stutters and frames up to 1.6 s. StreamText: 1–3 stutters, longest frame 17–21 ms.
- **A 1 MB burst** takes StreamText ~0.7 s to show in full, spread over frames on purpose (longest frame
  29 ms); the `setState` and Reanimated versions take 2.3–9 s and freeze for 0.9–1.8 s.
- **Mounting finished messages never drops a frame:** StreamText's longest frame is 9 ms (one 120 Hz frame) in
  every cell. `<Text>` takes 188 ms and an 83 ms frame for 20 × 5 KB, StreamText 68 ms.
- **Where StreamText costs more:** JS time in the commit, as on iOS (the layout is prepared there): 30 ms for
  50 KB and 40 ms for 20 × 5 KB, against 41 and 111 ms for `<Text>`. A single 5 KB message shows in 38 ms,
  later than the old text view path (12 ms), which lays it out on the main thread instead; the time is
  StaticLayout itself (2.5–14 ms for 5 KB between identical runs).
